# ICP & Persona Builder (Jobs-to-be-Done)

## Philosophy

Traditional personas describe who buyers are (demographics, job titles). JTBD personas describe what they're *trying to accomplish* — the functional, emotional, and social jobs they're hiring a product to do. JTBD produces better messaging, better sales conversations, and better product decisions.

The ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) is company-level. The Persona is person-level. Build both — they answer different questions.

---

## Part 1: ICP (Company-Level)

### ICP Scoring Criteria

Define your ICP by scoring firmographic and behavioral attributes. Weight each by how predictive it is of revenue, retention, and expansion.

| Attribute | What to define | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Industry / vertical** | Which sectors buy fastest and retain longest? | Signals domain fit and sales cycle |
| **Company size** | Headcount + revenue range | Signals deal size, complexity, and buying process |
| **Growth stage** | Startup / scale-up / enterprise | Signals urgency, budget authority, and risk tolerance |
| **Tech stack / infrastructure** | What tools do they already use? | Signals integration fit and switching cost |
| **Trigger events** | What's happening when they buy? | Funding, headcount growth, regulation, leadership change |
| **Budget ownership** | Who controls the budget? | Signals deal complexity and sales motion |
| **Geographic fit** | Where are they? | Regulatory, language, support constraints |

### ICP Scoring Template

Rate each account attribute on a 1–3 scale. Accounts scoring 18+ are ICP. 12–17 are near-ICP. Below 12 are out of profile.

## Part 2: Persona (Person-Level / JTBD)

### The JTBD Interview Structure

The best personas come from real buyer interviews. If you have them, extract the following. If not, use win/loss data or sales call recordings.

**Key questions to ask (or extract from existing data):**

1. **The hiring moment**: "Walk me through the moment you decided to look for a solution. What was happening?"
2. **The struggle**: "What were you trying to accomplish that you couldn't do before?"
3. **The alternatives**: "What did you try before? Why didn't it work?"
4. **The progress**: "What does success look like 6 months after implementing this?"
5. **The anxiety**: "What almost stopped you from buying?"
6. **The social job**: "How did you explain this purchase to your team / your boss?"

### Three Types of Jobs

| Job type | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Functional** | The practical task they need to accomplish | "I need to reduce time spent on manual reporting from 10hrs to 1hr/week" |
| **Emotional** | How they want to feel | "I want to feel confident presenting data to leadership, not anxious" |
| **Social** | How they want to be perceived | "I want to be seen as the person who brought AI into the team" |

All three matter. Messaging that only addresses the functional job leaves emotional and social purchase drivers on the table.

| Dimension | Content |

| **Success metric** | [How they'll know it's working, in their own words] |
| **Key anxiety** | [What almost stopped them from buying] |
| **Preferred channel** | [How they discover new tools: peer rec, LinkedIn, review sites, sales outreach] |
| **Champion potential** | [Will they expand, refer, or advocate? Why?] |

### Persona Validation Checklist

Before using a persona in messaging or sales enablement:
- [ ] Based on ≥5 real buyer interviews or equivalent win/loss data (not assumptions)
- [ ] Functional, emotional, and social jobs all defined
- [ ] "Struggling moment" is specific enough to use in ad copy or email subject lines
- [ ] At least one real buyer quote per job type
- [ ] Reviewed by someone in Sales who works these deals daily

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Building personas from internal assumptions | Interview at least 5 real buyers before finalizing |
| Defining only the functional job | Emotional and social jobs often drive the actual purchase decision |
| One persona for a broad market | Build separate personas for each distinct buying motion (e.g., self-serve vs. enterprise) |
| Conflating ICP (company) with persona (person) | ICP tells you which accounts to target; persona tells you who to talk to and what to say |
| Vague struggling moment | If you can't use it in an email subject line, it's not specific enough |
