# Sample Prompts for Product Launch Playbook

## Quick Start

Hey Claude — I just added the "product-launch-playbook" skill. I have a feature launch coming up and need help planning it.

## Use Cases

### Full Tier 1 Launch Plan

We're launching a completely new product line — an AI-powered analytics module that sits on top of our existing data platform. This is our first major product expansion in 2 years. Target launch date is April 15th. Our ICP is VP-level data leaders at mid-market SaaS companies. We're already in beta with 12 customers and have some early metrics. Can you build a complete Tier 1 launch plan including the brief, workback schedule, RACI, and comms plan?

### Tier 2 Feature Launch

We're launching a new Salesforce integration on March 1st — it's bi-directional sync with real-time updates. This was our #2 most-requested feature (189 votes on our roadmap board). Our main competitor launched their Salesforce integration 4 months ago, so we're playing catch-up. I need a Tier 2 launch plan with a 6-week workback. Budget for external marketing is limited so focus on owned channels.

### Quick Tier 3 Release Notes

We shipped 4 small improvements this sprint: keyboard shortcuts for power users, CSV export improvements, a new dark mode option, and faster page load times (40% improvement). Can you write release notes and an in-app notification? Keep it brief — these are minor but our users like knowing we're shipping.

### Launch Retrospective

We launched our new pricing model 45 days ago. Here's what we have so far:
- Target: 15% increase in ARPU → Actual: 8% increase
- Target: <5% churn spike → Actual: 7.2% churn in first month
- Sales team feedback: "The new tiers are confusing in competitive deals"
- Support tickets about pricing up 3x
- NPS dropped from 52 to 44

Can you run a structured launch retrospective and identify what went wrong and what to fix?

### Internal Comms Only

Our CEO wants to announce our Series C funding at the all-hands next Tuesday. I need: talking points for the CEO, an internal FAQ (people will ask about hiring, comp changes, etc.), and a template for the company-wide email. This is internal only — no press release yet.

## Tips for Best Results

- **State the launch tier** if you know it — or describe the scope and let the skill classify it
- **Share the timeline** — even rough dates help structure the workback
- **Name your constraints** — limited budget, small team, tight timeline, missing assets — all of these change the plan
- **Include competitive context** — if a competitor already has this feature, the messaging strategy changes significantly
- **Bring your beta data** — early adoption metrics, customer quotes, and usage data make the launch brief much stronger
- **Ask for specific pieces** — if you just need the comms plan or just the workback schedule, say so. You don't always need the full plan.
