# Competitive Intelligence — superseded

This module now defers to the standalone **`competitive-intelligence`** skill instead of
maintaining its own intel-gathering, competitor-profile, and battlecard templates here — see
the Module Registry and External Skills section in `SKILL.md`.

That skill covers both AppTweak competitors and competitors for any other product (Lucas's
outside-AppTweak work), includes the mechanism method for deriving durable structural
arguments (not just feature gaps), and hands off to `sales-enablement-assets` for building the
actual pitch materials once the battlecard is ready.

Keeping one living version there — rather than a duplicate copy in this file — is intentional:
it avoids the two drifting out of sync as the method improves.
