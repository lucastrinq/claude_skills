# Sample Prompts for Competitive Battlecard

## Quick Start

Hey Claude — I just added the "competitive-battlecard" skill. Can you help me create a battlecard against our top competitor?

## Use Cases

### Full Battlecard From Scratch

We're a project management platform for engineering teams (think Linear competitor). Our main competitor is ToolTrack — they've been winning deals in the mid-market segment. I need a complete competitive battlecard for our sales team. Here's what I know:

- We're stronger on developer experience, API, and integrations
- They're stronger on reporting and executive dashboards
- Their pricing is about 20% lower on paper but they charge for add-ons
- We've lost 3 enterprise deals to them this quarter

Can you build a full battlecard?

### Deal-Specific Competitive Brief

I have a $200K enterprise deal where the prospect is also evaluating DataVault. The champion is a VP of Engineering who cares about API reliability and SSO. The economic buyer is the CTO who's focused on total cost of ownership. Can you build a deal-specific competitive brief I can use in our next meeting on Thursday?

### Battlecard Update With New Intel

Our competitor CloudBase just announced a new analytics module and dropped their enterprise pricing by 15%. I need to update our existing battlecard to account for this. Here's the link to their announcement: [URL]. What sections need to change and what new trap questions or objection responses should we add?

### Quick Competitive Comparison

My rep just learned that Acme Platform is in our deal. I don't have a full battlecard for them yet. Can you do a quick 1-page competitive comparison I can use immediately? I know they're a smaller company, VC-funded Series B, focused on SMB, and their product is good for simple workflows but struggles with enterprise complexity.

## Tips for Best Results

- **Provide existing intel** — the more context you give (win/loss data, rep feedback, customer quotes), the more specific the battlecard will be
- **Name the persona** — a battlecard for enterprise AEs needs different content than one for SDRs or SEs
- **Share what you've lost on** — specific lost deal feedback is the highest-signal input
- **Ask for updates, not rewrites** — when competitive landscape changes, ask to update specific sections rather than regenerating the full card
- **Request a specific deal focus** when you have one — generic battlecards are useful but deal-specific ones close revenue
