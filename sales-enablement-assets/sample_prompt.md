# Sample Prompts for Sales Enablement Assets

## Quick Start

Hey Claude — I just added the "sales-enablement-assets" skill. I need to create some content for our sales team.

## Use Cases

### Solution One-Pager

I need a one-pager for our product, TaskFlow — a project management tool for marketing teams. Our main differentiator is template-driven workflows built specifically for marketing ops (campaign launches, content calendars, brand approvals). Target persona: VP of Marketing at companies with 50-200 employees. Key proof points: 68% faster campaign launches (from 3 customer case studies), integrations with HubSpot, Figma, and Slack. Main competitor is Monday.com. Can you draft a solution one-pager?

### Demo Script for Enterprise Deal

I have a demo next Wednesday with the CTO of a 2,000-person fintech company. From discovery, their top priorities are: SOC2 compliance, API-first architecture (they build custom integrations), and real-time collaboration for their distributed engineering team. They're also evaluating Competitor X. Can you write a demo script that leads with security/compliance (their biggest concern), builds to collaboration (our biggest differentiator), and handles the Competitor X comparison if it comes up?

### Objection Handling Guide

Our sales team keeps hearing the same 5-6 objections and everyone handles them differently. The biggest ones are: "We can build this ourselves," "Your competitor is cheaper," "We're not ready to switch tools," "How do I know this will work for our use case?", and "We need to see ROI in 90 days or we'll cancel." Can you build a structured objection handling guide with talk tracks and proof points for each?

### ROI Calculator

My champion at a prospect needs to justify a $75K annual investment to their CFO. They're a 300-person SaaS company with a 12-person customer support team. Their pain is that support agents spend ~40% of their time searching for answers across 5 different knowledge bases. Our product unifies knowledge search. Help me build an ROI business case with conservative, moderate, and aggressive scenarios.

### Full Sales Enablement Package

We're launching a new product tier called "Enterprise" in 6 weeks. The sales team needs: a product overview deck (10-12 slides), a solution one-pager targeting CISOs, a competitive comparison sheet vs. our top 2 competitors, and an objection handling guide for the pricing conversation (Enterprise is 3x our Pro tier). Can you help me build this full package? I'll provide our messaging framework and competitive intel.

## Tips for Best Results

- **Name the persona** — a one-pager for a VP of Engineering is totally different from one for a CFO
- **Share the deal stage** — early-stage content educates; late-stage content proves value
- **Include your proof points** — customer quotes, metrics, case studies. The skill can structure them, but you need to provide the raw evidence
- **Mention the competitor** — if there's a specific competitor in the deal, name them so the content can position against them
- **Be specific about format** — "one-pager" vs. "deck" vs. "script" vs. "guide" produces very different outputs
- **Provide brand voice guidelines if you have them** — tone matters. "Enterprise formal" and "startup casual" produce very different content
