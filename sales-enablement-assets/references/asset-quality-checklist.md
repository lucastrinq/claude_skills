# Sales Enablement Asset Quality Checklist

Score every asset before delivery. Check each item: Pass or Fail. Count total passes.

---

## Content Quality (5 checks)

### 1. Buyer-Centric Language
**Check:** The asset talks about the buyer's problems and outcomes more than your product's features. Count the ratio of "you/your" to "we/our" — it should be at least 2:1.

**Pass:** "You'll reduce manual categorization from 20 hours to 3 hours per week"
**Fail:** "Our AI-powered categorization engine uses proprietary NLP models"
**Why it matters:** Buyers care about their outcomes, not your architecture. Feature-first content gets ignored.

### 2. Every Claim Has Evidence
**Check:** Scan every assertion. Does each one have a proof point — a number, a customer quote, a third-party source, or verifiable data?

**Pass:** "Teams reduce feedback analysis time by 85% within 30 days (based on 47 customer implementations)"
**Fail:** "InsightFlow dramatically improves efficiency"
**Why it matters:** Unsupported claims are marketing fluff. Reps feel exposed using content they can't back up.

### 3. Appropriate Depth for the Stage
**Check:** Does the level of detail match the deal stage? Awareness content should be high-level and problem-focused. Decision content should be detailed and proof-focused.

**Pass:** An ROI calculator for a decision-stage deal with conservative/moderate/aggressive scenarios
**Fail:** A deep technical architecture document sent to someone still in awareness stage
**Why it matters:** Wrong depth = wrong conversation. Too detailed too early overwhelms; too shallow too late fails to convince.

### 4. Differentiation Is Specific
**Check:** Where you claim to be different, is the differentiation named, specific, and defensible? Or could you swap in any vendor name?

**Pass:** "Unlike [CompetitorA]'s rule-based categorization, InsightFlow uses contextual AI that understands sarcasm, mixed sentiment, and implicit feedback"
**Fail:** "We offer a superior, more intuitive solution than alternatives"
**Why it matters:** Vague differentiation is no differentiation. Prospects hear "better" from every vendor.

### 5. No Jargon Without Translation
**Check:** If you use a technical or industry term, is it immediately followed by a plain-language explanation?

**Pass:** "Real-time WebSocket connections — meaning changes appear instantly for everyone without refreshing"
**Fail:** "Leveraging our microservices-based architecture for optimal API throughput"
**Why it matters:** Even technical buyers appreciate clarity. Jargon signals that you're writing for yourself, not for them.

## Sales Usability (5 checks)

### 6. The 30-Second Scan Test
**Check:** Can a rep find the key takeaway in 30 seconds? Bold headings, bullet points, and visual hierarchy should guide the eye to the most important information.

**Pass:** Bold section headers, bullet points with bold lead phrases, key metrics highlighted
**Fail:** Wall of text paragraphs with no visual hierarchy
**Why it matters:** Reps reference content mid-call or skim before a meeting. If they can't find what they need in 30 seconds, they won't use it.

### 7. Customizable in Under 5 Minutes
**Check:** Can a rep personalize this for a specific deal quickly? Are there clear placeholders, modular sections, or easy-to-swap components?

**Pass:** "[Customer name]'s specific challenge with [pain point]" — clear brackets for personalization
**Fail:** A rigidly formatted document that only works for one exact scenario
**Why it matters:** Generic content gets used once. Customizable content gets used repeatedly. Every deal feels unique to the buyer.

### 8. Clear "When to Use" Guidance
**Check:** Does the asset include (or is it accompanied by) guidance on when in the sales process to use it and for which persona?

**Pass:** Header note: "Use after discovery call with technical buyers evaluating build vs. buy"
**Fail:** A great document with no context for when or how to deploy it
**Why it matters:** Even the best content fails when used at the wrong time or with the wrong audience.

### 9. Conversational Tone
**Check:** Could a rep say these words out loud without sounding like a press release? Read the talk tracks aloud — do they sound human?

**Pass:** "Here's the thing — most teams don't realize how much time they're spending on this until they measure it."
**Fail:** "Organizations leveraging our platform experience transformational efficiency gains."
**Why it matters:** Reps won't use content that makes them sound robotic. If they have to translate marketing-speak into human language, they'll just wing it.

### 10. Mobile-Readable
**Check:** Would this be readable if a rep opened it on their phone before walking into a meeting?

**Pass:** Short paragraphs, large headings, scannable bullet points
**Fail:** Dense tables, tiny fonts, landscape-only layouts, multi-column designs
**Why it matters:** Reps review content on their phones in the elevator, in the Uber, in the lobby. If it doesn't work on mobile, it doesn't work.

## Effectiveness (5 checks)

### 11. Addresses the Top Objection
**Check:** Does the asset preemptively address or include responses for the most common objection at this deal stage?

**Pass:** A one-pager that includes a "Common Questions" section addressing "How is this different from [DIY/free alternative]?"
**Fail:** Content that ignores the elephant in the room — the buyer is definitely thinking about it even if the one-pager doesn't mention it
**Why it matters:** Unaddressed objections don't go away. They fester. Better to name them and neutralize them.

### 12. Single, Clear CTA
**Check:** Does the asset end with exactly ONE clear next step? Not "learn more" — a specific action with a specific path.

**Pass:** "Schedule a 30-minute technical evaluation → [calendar link] or reply to your AE"
**Fail:** "Learn more at our website" / No CTA at all / Five CTAs competing for attention
**Why it matters:** Every piece of content should move the deal forward. If the reader finishes and doesn't know what to do next, you wasted the moment.

### 13. Competitive Awareness
**Check:** Does the asset position against likely alternatives without being overtly negative? Does it create evaluation criteria that favor you?

**Pass:** "When evaluating solutions, look for: contextual AI (not just keyword matching), native integrations (not Zapier-dependent), and team health dashboards (not just ticket counts)" — criteria that describe your strengths
**Fail:** Ignoring competition entirely, or "CompetitorX sucks because..."
**Why it matters:** Buyers are comparing you to alternatives whether you acknowledge it or not. Shape the criteria.

### 14. Proof Points Are Relevant to the Audience
**Check:** Are the customer stories, metrics, and quotes from companies that look like the prospect? Same industry, similar size, comparable use case?

**Pass:** Showing a 500-person SaaS company's results to a 400-person SaaS company prospect
**Fail:** Showing an enterprise Fortune 500 case study to an SMB prospect
**Why it matters:** Prospects need to see themselves in your proof points. Irrelevant social proof is almost as bad as no social proof.

### 15. No Dead Ends
**Check:** If a reader wants to go deeper on any claim, is there a path? Links to case studies, help docs, or "ask your AE about X."

**Pass:** Key claims have parenthetical links or footnotes for deeper investigation
**Fail:** Bold claims with no way to verify or explore further
**Why it matters:** Sophisticated buyers want to validate. Give them the path to do so, and you build trust while they sell themselves.

---

## Scoring

| Passes | Verdict | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **13-15** | **Ship it.** This asset is ready for sales. | Deliver to the user. Note any areas scored as "Fail" for optional improvement. |
| **9-12** | **Needs revision.** Good foundation, specific issues to fix. | Revise failed items before delivery. Prioritize Effectiveness failures first. |
| **5-8** | **Major rework.** Too many gaps to patch — restructure the asset. | Identify the root issue (wrong audience? wrong stage? wrong format?) and rebuild. |
| **Below 5** | **Start over.** Fundamental misalignment. | Re-clarify the need in Phase 1 before attempting another draft. |
