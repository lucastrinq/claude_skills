# Sample Prompts for Win/Loss Analyzer

## Quick Start

Hey Claude — I just added the "win-loss-analyzer" skill. I have some deal data I'd like analyzed.

## Use Cases

### Full Analysis From CRM Export

I have a CSV export of our Q4 closed deals — 180 opportunities with outcome, deal size, competitor, loss reason, and sales cycle data. Can you run a full win/loss analysis? I need an executive summary for our Q1 QBR and a separate product insights report for our PM team.

Here's the file: [attach CSV]

### Single Deal Deep-Dive (Transcript Analysis)

We just lost a $150K enterprise deal to CompetitorX. I have the Gong recording transcript from our final call and the prospect's email explaining their decision. Can you analyze what went wrong and identify the root cause? I want to know if this was a product gap, sales execution issue, or something else.

[paste transcript]

### Competitive Loss Deep-Dive

We've lost 8 deals to RivalPlatform this quarter. I have the CRM data for all of them plus interview notes from 3 of the buyers. Can you do a competitive loss deep-dive? I want to understand: are we losing on product, price, or perception — and what specifically should we change?

### Win/Loss Interview Design

We're about to start a formal win/loss interview program. We close about 50 deals per quarter. Can you help me design the program? I need: which deals to prioritize for interviews, the interview guide, and a coding framework for analyzing the results.

### Quarterly Trend Analysis

Here are our win/loss numbers for the last 4 quarters. Can you identify trends, flag anything concerning, and tell me what's improving vs. getting worse? I want this framed as "here's what leadership needs to know."

Q1: 58W / 42L / 12ND — $2.8M won
Q2: 62W / 38L / 15ND — $3.1M won
Q3: 55W / 48L / 18ND — $2.9M won
Q4: 51W / 52L / 22ND — $2.6M won

## Tips for Best Results

- **Provide structured data when possible** — CSV exports from your CRM produce the most rigorous analysis
- **Include deal size** — revenue-weighted analysis often tells a different story than count-based
- **Name your competitors** — specific competitive analysis requires knowing who was in each deal
- **Share the audience** — an executive summary is different from a product insights report; tell me who'll read it
- **Combine quant + qual** — the best insights come from pairing CRM data with interview transcripts or call recordings
- **Be honest about sample sizes** — if you only have 20 deals, I'll tell you what we can and can't conclude from that
