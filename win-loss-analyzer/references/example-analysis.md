# Win/Loss Analysis — Q3 Executive Summary

**Company:** NexGen CRM
**Period:** Q3 2025 (July–September)
**Analyst:** PMM Team
**Data sources:** Salesforce CRM export (250 closed opps), 18 win/loss interviews, 12 Gong call recordings
**Confidence level:** High (robust sample size with both quantitative and qualitative data)

---

| Metric | Q3 Value | Q2 Value | Trend |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overall win rate | 50.8% (127/250) | 54.2% | ▼ -3.4pp |

**Key insight:** We're winning more small deals but losing more large deals. Average lost deal size jumped 21.7%, meaning the revenue impact of losses is growing faster than the count suggests. Competitive win rate declined nearly 6 points — the most significant single-quarter drop in 4 quarters.

**RivalCRM is the critical threat.** 18 losses at 35.7% win rate, concentrated in deals >$75K. Win/loss interviews reveal their new analytics module (launched July) is shifting evaluation criteria in enterprise deals. This was not a factor in Q2.

> "Their dashboards are just better. My CFO saw the RivalCRM demo and asked why ours couldn't do that." — Lost deal, $95K, Enterprise (interview #7)

> "We liked NexGen's workflow automation more, but the executive team made the call based on reporting capabilities." — Lost deal, $120K, Enterprise (interview #12)

## Win Themes (From Won Deals)

From 18 win/loss interviews and 12 Gong recordings, the top reasons customers chose us:

1. **Workflow automation depth** (cited 14/18 times): "Nobody else lets us automate this many steps without engineering resources"
2. **Time to value** (cited 11/18): "We were live in 2 weeks. Their competitor quoted 8 weeks for implementation"
3. **Customer support quality** (cited 9/18): "The support team actually understood our use case and helped us configure it"
4. **API flexibility** (cited 7/18): "We could integrate with everything we needed through the API"

> "We chose NexGen because we were up and running in 10 days. RivalCRM quoted us 6-8 weeks and wanted $30K for implementation." — Won deal, $65K, Mid-Market

## No-Decision Analysis

34 deals (13.6%) ended in no-decision, up from 10.8% in Q2.

| No-Decision Reason | Count | Revenue |
|---|---|---|
| Status quo won (kept current process) | 14 | $310K |
| Budget freeze / competing priority | 9 | $240K |
| Consensus failure (committee couldn't agree) | 7 | $180K |
| Champion left / reorg | 4 | $95K |

**Status quo is our biggest "competitor."** 14 deals worth $310K chose to do nothing. These are the most addressable no-decisions — the buyers had the problem, saw the solution, and still didn't act. Sales process analysis suggests insufficient urgency creation during discovery.

## Top 3 Recommendations

### 1. Accelerate Enterprise Reporting Capability (Product)

**The problem:** Enterprise win rate dropped 8.2pp, primarily driven by RivalCRM's new analytics module. 8 lost deals worth $780K cited reporting as the deciding factor.

**The recommendation:** Prioritize the dashboard/reporting overhaul for Q1 ship. Doesn't need feature parity with RivalCRM — needs to clear the "good enough" bar for enterprise buyers who currently see it as a disqualifier.

**Expected impact:** If we convert even 30% of the reporting-related losses, that's ~$234K in recovered revenue per quarter and a 3-4pp improvement in enterprise win rate.

**Owner:** VP Product

### 2. Overhaul Enterprise Sales Motion (Sales Enablement)

**The problem:** Enterprise deals take 52 days when lost vs. 34 when won, and our enterprise win rate (30%) is half our SMB rate (66.7%). We're losing on multi-threading — 11/18 enterprise losses had a single point of contact.

**The recommendation:** Implement mandatory multi-threading for deals >$75K: require 3+ contacts in the opportunity by Stage 3. Create an enterprise-specific demo script that leads with reporting capabilities (neutralize the weakness) and builds to workflow automation (play to our strength). Update the RivalCRM battlecard with the new analytics module competitive response.

**Expected impact:** Industry benchmark for multi-threaded enterprise deals is 40-50% win rate. Moving from 30% to 40% on our enterprise pipeline would add ~$340K per quarter.

**Owner:** VP Sales + PMM

### 3. Build Urgency Playbook for No-Decision Reduction (PMM + Sales)

**The problem:** No-decision rate climbed to 13.6%, with $310K lost to status quo alone. Buyers see the problem but don't feel enough urgency to act.

**The recommendation:** Create a "cost of inaction" calculator and talk track for discovery calls. Arm reps with industry benchmarks on what status quo costs (time, money, risk). Build a customer story specifically for the "we almost did nothing" narrative — a customer who considered staying with their current process and what it cost them.

**Expected impact:** Reducing no-decision rate from 13.6% to 10% would redirect ~25 deals back into the pipeline. Even at our average win rate, that's ~$600K in potential annual revenue.

**Owner:** PMM

## Data Quality Notes

- CRM data is complete for all 250 opportunities. Loss reason was populated by the rep in 82% of cases; remaining 18% were coded by PMM based on notes.
- Interview sample (18) represents 20% of lost deals. Potential selection bias: reps may have referred more cooperative contacts.
- Gong recordings were available for 43% of lost deals. Analysis focused on final calls and discovery calls.
- Competitive data is based on our knowledge and public sources as of September 2025. RivalCRM's analytics module assessment is based on 3 demo recordings shared by prospects and 6 G2 reviews.
- All financial figures are ACV, not TCV.
